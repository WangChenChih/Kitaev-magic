\section{Stabilizer R\'enyi Entropy of the Kitaev Ground State}
The \textbf{Kitaev ground state is a stabilizer state} due to the Wilson symmetry, and the \textbf{stabilizer group is constituted by the plaquette operators} $\anglelr{\lrg{W_p}}$. The stabilizer projector can also be understood as a \textit{condensation operator} proposed in Ref.~\cite{Choi-Sanghavi-Shao-Zheng_2025}, which is related to the non-invertible symmetry in a two-dimensional system. The ground state density matrix reads~\cite{wang2026exactorganizationdensitymatrices}
\[
\ket{\mrm{Gnd}}\bra{\mrm{Gnd}} 
= 
\frac{1}{2^{N-N_p}} 
\lr{\frac{\mathds{1} + \tilde{W}_v}{2}} 
\lr{\frac{\mathds{1} + \tilde{W}_h}{2}}
\prod_{p} \lr{\frac{\mathds{1} + W_p}{2}}
\sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]} \mathfrak{C}_{\mathfrak{D}} \Sigma_{\mathfrak{D}}
\]
where $\mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D} \equiv \eta_\mathfrak{D} \anglelr{\Sigma_\mathfrak{D}}$. Since a pure-state density matrix is a projector, the ground state $\ket{\mrm{Gnd}}$ can be obtained by acting the density matrix on an arbitrary state $\ket{\phi}$
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gnd-as-stab}
    \ket{\mrm{Gnd}}
    =
    C(\phi)
    \underbrace{
    \lr{\frac{\mathds{1} + \tilde{W}_v}{2}} 
    \lr{\frac{\mathds{1} + \tilde{W}_h}{2}}
    \prod_{p} \lr{\frac{\mathds{1} + W_p}{2}}
    }_{\text{gauge ($=$stabilizer)}}
    \underbrace{
    \lrm{
        \frac{1}{2^{N - N_p}}
        \sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]} \mathfrak{C}_{\mathfrak{D}}
        \Sigma_\mathfrak{D}
    }
    }_{\text{matter}}
    \ket{\phi}
\end{equation}
where $C(\phi) \equiv 2^{N_p-N}\braket{\mrm{Gnd}}{\phi}^{-1}$ is a normalization coefficient depending on the initial state. The above form of a state is reminiscent of a state in a \textit{stabilizer space}. A stabilizer space is a subspace in which the states are the eigenvectors of the elements in a \textit{stabilizer group} with eigenvalues $+1$. Note that if the dimension of a stabilizer space is one, we call the only state in such a stabilizer space a \textit{stabilizer state}. The generators $\lrg{g_i}$, which are commuting $\mathbb{Z}_2$ operators, generate a \textit{stabilizer group} denoted by $\anglelr{\lrg{g_i}}$. The projector that projects states onto the stabilizer space $\mathcal{V}_S$ is 
\[
\hat{\Pi} \equiv C\sum_{g\in\mathcal{V}_S} g
\]
with a specific normalization constant $C$. In the Kitaev model, the plaquette operators $\lrg{W_p}$ serve as the generators of a stabilizer space $\anglelr{\lrg{W_p}}$, and the projector is exactly the projector preceding Eq.~\eqref{eq:gnd-as-stab}
\[
\hat{\Pi}
=
\lr{\frac{\mathds{1} + \tilde{W}_v}{2}} 
\lr{\frac{\mathds{1} + \tilde{W}_h}{2}}
\prod_{p} \lr{\frac{\mathds{1} + W_p}{2}}.
\]
In other words, \textit{the stabilizer space generated by the plaquette operators and the global Wilson loop operators is the flux-free subspace in the topological sector labeled by the number pair $(\tilde{W}_v, \tilde{W}_h)$}. Therefore, the dimension of such a stabilizer space is 
\[
\dim(\mathcal{V}_S) = \Tr(\hat{\Pi}) = 2^{N - N_p - 1}.
\]
Although the stabilizer space is not strictly one-dimensional, the model has only one ground state independent of the initial state. This implies that the matter part in Eq.~\eqref{eq:gnd-as-stab} reduces the degrees of freedom through a mechanism different from the stabilizer projector. In the following discussion, we provide a physical picture where \textit{the matter part is the non-stabilizerness of the ground state by examining the form of the stabilizer R\'enyi entropy}. 

The $n$-\textit{stabilizer R\'enyi entropy} is defined as~\cite{Leone-Oliviero-Hamma_2022},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:stab-renyi-def}
    M_{n}\lr{\ket{\psi}} := 
    \frac{1}{1 - n} 
    \ln\lr{
        \sum_{\mathsf{S}\in\tilde{\mathcal{P}}_N}
        \frac{1}{2^N}
        \Tr\lr{\rho\mathsf{S}}^{2n}
    }
\end{equation}
where $\rho = \ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}$ is the pure state density matrix of $\ket{\psi}$. We need to bear in mind that the definition above given in Ref.~\cite{Leone-Oliviero-Hamma_2022} applies strictly to pure states. For mixed states, such a definition will not be a valid monotone. For the Kitaev model, the stabilizer R\'enyi entropy can be calculated straightforwardly as follows
\begin{equation}\label{eq:genral-SRE}
    M_{n}\lr{\ket{\psi}} 
    = 
    \frac{1}{1 - n} 
    \ln\lr{
        \frac{1}{2^N} \sum_{\mathsf{S}\in\tilde{\mathcal{P}}_N}
        \anglelr{\mathsf{S}}^{2n}
    } 
    =
    \frac{1}{1 - n} 
    \ln\lr{
        \frac{1}{2^{N+1}}
        \sum_{\mathfrak{D}}
        \eta_\mathfrak{D}^n
        \mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}^{2n}
    } 
\end{equation}
In the derivation, we need to be careful that $\Sigma_{\mathfrak{D}_{zz}} \propto \Sigma_{\mathfrak{D}_Z}$. Owing to this, we have to divide the summation by two to avoid overcounting. Here we demonstrate the proof of the property that $\anglelr{\mathsf{S}}^2 = \eta_\mathfrak{D}\mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}^2$ if $\mathsf{S} \propto \Sigma_\mathfrak{D}$, which has been used in the above derivation.  Suppose $\Sigma_\mathfrak{D} = c\mathsf{S}$ with $c$ a specific constant. By definition, the phase $\eta_{\mathfrak{D}}$ is given by $\eta_\mathfrak{D} = \anglelr{\Sigma_\mathfrak{D}^2}$. Substituting the assumption into the definition of $\eta_\mathfrak{D}$ gives
\[
\anglelr{\Sigma_\mathfrak{D}^2} = \eta_\mathfrak{D} = \anglelr{c^2\mathsf{S}^2} = c^2
\]
With this result, we ultimately have the following.
\[
\anglelr{\mathsf{S}}^2 = \frac{1}{c^2}\anglelr{\Sigma_\mathfrak{D}}^2 = \eta_\mathfrak{D}\mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}^2
\]
This relation proves Eq.~\eqref{eq:genral-SRE}. The expectation value of the Pauli strings can be obtained by evaluating the Pfaffian of the correlation matrix defined by Eq.~\eqref{eq:corr-mat-def}
\[
\anglelr{\mathsf{S}}^2 = \mrm{Pf}\lr{\mathsf{C}_\mathfrak{D}}^2
.
\]
Next, we exploit the organization of the density matrix to simplify the form of the SRE. Since both $\eta_\mathfrak{D}$ and $\mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}$ depend only on equivalent classes of the strings, we have
\[
\sum_{\mathfrak{D}} \eta_\mathfrak{D}^n \mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}^{2n}
=
2^{N_p + 1}
\sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]} \eta_\mathfrak{D}^n
\mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}^{2n}
\]
The factor $2^{N_p+1}$ comes from the number of flux sectors. 
\[
\frac{\dim(\mathcal{H})}{\dim(\hat{\Pi})}
=
\frac{2^N}{2^{N-N_p-1}} = 2^{N_p + 1}
\]
Substituting this result into the SRE formula, we obtain
\begin{equation}
    M_n\lr{\ket{\mrm{Gnd}}} 
    =
    \frac{1}{1 - n}
    \ln\lr{
        \frac{1}{2^{N - N_p}}
        \sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]} \eta_\mathfrak{D}^n \mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}^{2n}
    }
\end{equation}
As one would expect, the $n = 1$ stabilizer R\'enyi entropy is an indeterminate form due to the normalization condition Eq.~\eqref{eq:weak-condition}. Therefore, computing the $n=1$ entropy requires taking the limit.
\[
    M_1(\ket{\mrm{Gnd}}) 
    = 
    \lim_{n\to 1} 
    \frac{1}{1 - n}
    \ln\lr{
        \frac{1}{2^{N - N_p}}
        \sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]} \eta_\mathfrak{D}^n \mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}^{2n}
    }
    =
    -\frac{1}{2^{N-N_p}}
    \sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]} \eta_\mathfrak{D}\mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}^2
    \ln\lr{\eta_\mathfrak{D}\mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}^2}
\]
\textbf{In the toric code limit}, all non-zero coefficients $\mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D} = 1$ reduce to unity, \textbf{rendering $M_n = 0$}~\footnote{Note that there are $2^{N_d} = 2^{N/2} = 2^{N-N_p}$ configurations $[\mathfrak{D}]$ that give finite correlation functions $\mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}$ in the toric code limit.}. This implies that the ground state is a perfect stabilizer state in the toric code limit, in agreement with our knowledge of the toric code ground state and its role as a stabilizer state. Eq.~\eqref{eq:genral-SRE} shows that the stabilizer R\'enyi entropy depends only on the equivalence class of the edge configurations and the Majorana fermionic correlation functions, implying that the matter sector governs the magic resource of the ground state. Therefore, it can be sampled using fermionic techniques~\cite{Wang-Yang-Zhou-Chen_2025}. Fig.~\ref{fig:SRE-numerics} shows numerical results for the stabilizer R\'enyi entropy.

\section{Stabilizer R\'enyi Entropy for Reduced Density Matrices}
The stabilizer R\'enyi entropy can be extended to general mixed states~\cite{Leone-Oliviero-Hamma_2022}.
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mixed-SRE-def}
    \tilde{M}_2\lr{\rho} = 
    -\ln\lr{
            \frac{1}{2^N}
            \sum_{\mathsf{S}\in\tilde{\mathcal{P}}_N}
            \Tr\lr{\rho\mathsf{S}}^{4}
        }
    - R_2\lr{\rho}
\end{equation}
where $R_2\lr{\rho} \equiv -\ln\lr{\Tr\rho^2}$ is the 2-R\'enyi entropy of the state $\rho$. For the Kitaev model, the reduced density matrix of a subregion $A$ reads~\cite{wang2026exactorganizationdensitymatrices}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:red-den-mat-sim-con}
    \rho_A = \frac{1}{2^{N^A-N^A_p}} \hat{\Pi}_A\sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]\subset A}\mathfrak{C}_{\mathfrak{D}}\Sigma_{\mathfrak{D}}.
\end{equation}
The 2-R\'enyi entropy can be calculated straightforwardly,
\begin{align*}
    \Tr_A\rho_A^2
    &=
    \frac{1}{2^{2\lr{N^A - N^A_p}}}
        \sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]\subset A}  \sum_{[\mathfrak{D}']\subset A} \mathfrak{C}_{\mathfrak{D}} \mathfrak{C}_{\mathfrak{D}'} 
        \Tr_A\lr{
            \hat{\Pi}_A 
            \Sigma_{\mathfrak{D}} \Sigma_{\mathfrak{D}'}
        } \\
    &= 
    \frac{1}{2^{2\lr{N^A - N^A_p}}}
        \sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]\subset A}  \sum_{[\mathfrak{D}']\subset A} 
        \vartheta\lr{\mathfrak{D},\mathfrak{D}'}
        \mathfrak{C}_{\mathfrak{D}} \mathfrak{C}_{\mathfrak{D}'} 
        \Tr_A\lr{
            \hat{\Pi}_A 
            \Sigma_{\mathfrak{D}\ominus\mathfrak{D}'}
        } \\
    &=
    \frac{1}{2^{2\lr{N^A - N^A_p}}}
        \sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]\subset A}  \eta_\mathfrak{D}
        \mathfrak{C}_{\mathfrak{D}}^2 
        \Tr_A  \hat{\Pi}_A  \\
    &= 
    \frac{1}{2^{N^A - N^A_p}}
        \sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]\subset A}
        \eta_\mathfrak{D} \mathfrak{C}_{\mathfrak{D}}^2. 
\end{align*}
The first term of Eq.~\eqref{eq:mixed-SRE-def} can also be expressed in terms of the coefficients $\mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}$.
\[
    \frac{1}{2^{N^A}}
        \sum_{\mathsf{S}\in\tilde{\mathcal{P}}_{N^A}}
        \lrm{\Tr_A\lr{\rho_A\mathsf{S}}}^{4}
    =
    \frac{1}{2^{N^A}}
    \sum_{\mathsf{S}\in\tilde{\mathcal{P}}_{N^A}}
        \anglelr{\mathsf{S}}^{4}
    =
    \frac{1}{2^{N^A}}
    \sum_{\mathfrak{D}\subset A}
        \eta_\mathfrak{D}^2\mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}^{4}
    =
    \frac{1}{2^{N^A-N^A_p}}
    \sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]\subset A}  \mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}^{4}
\]
Together, the SRE for the reduced density matrix reduces to the following form,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:RDM-SRE}
    \tilde{M}_2\lr{\rho_A} = 
    -\ln\lr{
        \frac{
        \sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]\subset A} \mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}^4 
        }
        {
        \sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]\subset A} \eta_\mathfrak{D} \mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}^2
        } 
    }.
\end{equation}
This form has already been proposed in Ref.~\cite{Wang-Yang-Zhou-Chen_2025}.

The R\'enyi entropy (entanglement entropy) part can be calculated numerically by diagonalizing the correlation matrix of the Majorana fermions in the subsystem $A$.
\[
    \sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]\subset A} \eta_\mathfrak{D} \mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}^2
    =
    \sum_{\mrm{EP}(\mathfrak{D}) \subset R} \lrm{\mrm{Pf}(\mathsf{C}_\mathfrak{D})}^2 
    =
    \sum_{\mrm{EP}(\mathfrak{D}) \subset R} \det(\mathsf{C}_\mathfrak{D})
    = 
    \det\lr{\mathds{1} + \mathsf{C}_A}
\]

\[
\Gamma_A = \frac{1}{2}\mathds{1} + \frac{i}{2} \mathsf{C}_A
\]
Since the spectrum of the correlation matrix $\Gamma_A$ is the single-particle entanglement spectrum 
\[
\gamma_{k,\pm} = \frac{1 \pm \nu_{k}}{2} \;, \qquad k = 1,2,\cdots, \frac{N^A}{2}
\]
of the reduced density matrix $\rho_A$ of the subregion $A$, the eigenvalues of $\mathsf{C}_A$ are 
\begin{equation}\label{eq:CA-eigen}
    \pm i\frac{\nu_k}{2} \;,\qquad k = 1,2,\cdots, \frac{N^A}{2}.
\end{equation}
Therefore, the 2-R\'enyi entropy reduces to
\begin{align*}
    -\ln\lr{ \frac{1}{2^{N^A-N^A_p}} \sum_{[\mathfrak{D}]\subset A} \mathfrak{C}_\mathfrak{D}^2}
    &=
    -\ln\det\lr{\mathds{1} + \mathsf{C}_A} + \lr{N^A - N^A_p}\ln 2 \\
    &=
    -\ln\lrm{\prod_{k = 1}^{N^A/2} \frac{1}{2}\lr{1 + i\nu_k}\lr{1 - i\nu_k} } + \lr{\frac{N^A}{2} - N^A_p}\ln 2 \\
    &= -\sum_{k=1}^{N^A/2}\ln\lr{\frac{1 + \nu_k^2}{2}} + \lr{\frac{1}{2}\abs{\de A} - 1}\ln 2 
\end{align*}
